CBSE Class 9 AI Practice Sample Paper

Time: 1 Hour
Marks: 30

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions
1. What does ICT stand for?
2. Write one example of a digital footprint.
3. Name one non-verbal communication method.
4. What is self-awareness?
5. Write one quality of an entrepreneur.

Section B: Short Answer Questions
1. Explain two benefits of ICT in education.
2. Write two safe internet practices.
3. How does time management help a student?
4. What is active listening?
5. Why is feedback important in communication?

Section C: Application Questions
1. A student has many assignments and feels stressed. Suggest three self-management steps.
2. Your class wants to create a small useful product for school. Write the problem, possible solution, and one customer need.
3. Explain how online learning can be useful and mention one risk that students should avoid.
