CBSE Class 9 AI - Employability Skills Revision Notes

Communication Skills
Communication is the process of sharing information so that another person can understand it. It includes speaking, listening, writing, reading, gestures, facial expressions, and visual signs.

Good communication habits:
- Listen before replying.
- Use simple and respectful words.
- Keep your message clear and complete.
- Ask questions when something is unclear.
- Give feedback politely.

Self-Management
Self-management means controlling your time, habits, emotions, and actions responsibly. A self-managed student plans work, stays organised, and improves through practice.

Important points:
- Self-awareness helps you know your strengths and weak areas.
- Self-confidence helps you try tasks without fear.
- Time management helps you finish important work first.
- Goal setting gives direction to your effort.

ICT and Cyber Safety
ICT includes digital tools such as computers, smartphones, internet, email, and online learning platforms. It helps students learn faster and communicate easily.

Safe ICT habits:
- Use strong passwords.
- Do not share OTPs or private information.
- Avoid unknown links and downloads.
- Log out from shared computers.
- Think before posting online.

Entrepreneurial Skills
Entrepreneurial skills help people identify needs and create useful solutions. Students can use these skills for school projects, problem solving, teamwork, and planning.

Quick revision:
- Communication improves understanding.
- Self-management builds discipline.
- ICT connects people and information.
- Entrepreneurship turns ideas into action.
