# TechLearners Monetization Checklist

## Completed in the website

- Public About, Author, Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, Disclaimer, and Editorial Policy pages
- Visible legal links in navigation and the permanent footer
- Search sitemap and robots rules
- Canonical URLs and page-specific descriptions for public pages
- `noindex` tags for login, dashboard, and admin pages
- Homepage WebSite structured data and social sharing metadata
- Optional Google Analytics loading only after a visitor allows analytics cookies
- Privacy Policy disclosure for analytics and advertising cookies

## Before applying for Google AdSense

1. Publish original notes, explanations, and revision material across the subject pages. Avoid thin pages that only contain links or embedded videos.
2. Check that every lecture page adds your own written summary or learning guidance around embedded videos.
3. Create a Google Search Console property for `https://www.techlearners.in/` and submit `https://www.techlearners.in/sitemap.xml`.
4. Review Search Console indexing and Core Web Vitals reports after Google crawls the site.
5. Apply for AdSense only after the site has enough useful original content for a manual review.

## After AdSense provides your publisher ID

1. Add the AdSense verification script provided by Google.
2. Add `/ads.txt` using the exact publisher ID from the AdSense account.
3. Configure a Google-certified consent management platform for visitors in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland. Google's Privacy & messaging tool is one available option.
4. Confirm the Privacy Policy still accurately describes the advertising vendors enabled in the AdSense account.
