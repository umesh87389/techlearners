What was expanded?

Original note on techlearners.in (Part A: Employability Skills - Class 10 IT Complete Notes) was ~4,100 characters / 39 lines – only 5 bullet points per unit. This version expands it to full NCERT/CBSE session-wise notes:

  • Unit 1 – Communication Skills-II: methods, cycle, feedback types (descriptive/specific/non-specific), barriers, 7Cs, writing skills, sentences & punctuation.
  • Unit 2 – Self-Management-II: stress, stressors, ABC model, 5-step coping, time-management quadrants, independent work, self-awareness/motivation/regulation.
  • Unit 3 – ICT Skills-II: OS functions & types, file system, extensions, maintenance (hardware/software), virus/malware, firewall, backup 3-2-1, disk tools.
  • Unit 4 – Entrepreneurial Skills-II: entrepreneur/entrepreneurship/enterprise, characteristics, functions, role in society & myths vs realities, attitudes & values.
  • Unit 5 – Green Skills-II: sustainable development, 3 pillars, all 17 SDGs, green economy sectors, green jobs, 4Rs + Recover, individual actions & green projects.
