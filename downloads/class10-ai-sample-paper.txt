CBSE Class 10 AI Practice Sample Paper

Time: 1 Hour
Marks: 30

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions
1. What is feedback?
2. Name one stress-management technique.
3. What is a computer virus?
4. Write one green skill habit.
5. What is problem scoping in an AI project?

Section B: Short Answer Questions
1. Explain two barriers to communication.
2. Why is goal setting useful?
3. Write two file-management practices.
4. How can entrepreneurs use customer feedback?
5. Why should an AI solution be evaluated?

Section C: Application Questions
1. A student wants to prepare for exams without stress. Suggest a weekly self-management plan.
2. Your school wants to reduce electricity waste. Write three green actions.
3. Choose a simple AI problem from daily life and describe the problem, data needed, and success measure.
