Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Information We Collect

TechLearners uses Firebase Authentication for student accounts. When you create an account, Firebase processes your email address and password securely. Passwords are not stored by TechLearners.

If you use the contact form, we store your name, email address and message so the site administrator can respond.

How We Use Information

We use account information to provide access to the student dashboard and contact messages to answer questions or feedback.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Google Firebase for authentication and database services, GitHub Pages for hosting, and may embed YouTube lectures.

Your Choices

To request deletion of your account or contact message, use the Contact Us page.
