3 Technology Teams & The Activity Cycle

Each team has one clear job, produces tangible portfolio deliverables, and follows the simple 4-step cycle: Learn → Make → Show → Record.

💻 IT Team 🤖 Robotics Team 🧠 AI Team ⭐ The 4-Step Cycle
Standard Operating Flow

Simple Team Activity Cycle

For every team and every student project: keeps the workload manageable and generates authentic evidence for the student's digital portfolio.

1
📖

Learn

Students explore key concepts, learn design rules, study circuit logic, or understand how AI models work.

Example: Learn Canva layouts, sensor wiring, or image classification datasets.
➔
2
🛠️

Make

Students build hands-on digital works, assemble working circuits, or train real AI models.

Example: Create 3-5 posters/slides, assemble a soil sensor bot, or train a waste classifier.
➔
3
🎤

Show

Students demonstrate their work live in front of the class, teacher, or school science expo.

Example: Present slides in assembly, demonstrate robot in lab, or test AI camera live.
➔
4
📹

Record

Students record a 1-minute video demo and capture photos/screenshots for their digital portfolio.

Example: 60-second video demo + photo evidence published to the verified portfolio.
💻
Track 1

IT Team

Work: Create Digital Content

🎯 Final Output: Best 3–5 digital works + photos + short team video

📋 What IT Team Students Do:

  • 🎨 Make posters (Event announcements, science awareness, clubs)
  • 📊 Make presentations (Class seminar decks, project pitches)
  • 📄 Create documents (Newsletters, handbooks, guidelines)
  • 📈 Make spreadsheets (Budget calculators, grade/attendance charts)
  • 📢 Prepare school/event notices (Official circulars, digital bulletin boards)

Track 2

Robotics Team

Work: Build and demonstrate robots/models

🎯 Final Output: 1–3 working projects + photos + demonstration video

📋 What Robotics Team Students Do:

  • ⚡ Make simple circuits (LED sequencers, buzzers, breadboard wiring)
  • 📡 Work with sensors (Moisture probes, ultrasonic sonar, light LDRs)
  • 🤖 Build small robots (Line followers, obstacle avoidance rovers)
  • 💻 Program robots (Micro:bit block code, Arduino C++)
  • 🔬 Demonstrate working models (Live arena races & STEM expo showcases)

Track 3

AI Team

Work: Explore and create simple AI projects

🎯 Final Output: 1–3 AI projects + screenshots/photos + short team video

📋 What AI Team Students Do:

  • 📚 Learn about AI (Neural concepts, machine vision, prompt design)
  • ⚙️ Use AI tools (Teachable Machine, TensorFlow.js, Speech APIs)
  • 💡 Create simple AI projects (Garbage sorters, botanical health scanners)
  • 📷 Make image/text recognition projects (Hand gesture bots, sentiment detectors)
  • 🎤 Give AI demonstrations (Interactive classroom tests & live camera demos)
