UNIT 1: COMMUNICATION SKILLS - II
1. Methods of Communication:
   - Verbal: Oral (face-to-face, phone) and Written (letters, emails, reports).
   - Non-Verbal: Body language, facial expressions, gestures, postures, eye contact, paralanguage (tone, pitch).
   - Visual: Graphs, signs, maps, charts, diagrams.
2. Communication Cycle: Sender -> Message -> Encoding -> Channel -> Receiver -> Decoding -> Feedback.
3. Feedback: Essential to complete cycle. Types: Descriptive (detailed advice), Specific (pinpoints exact point), Non-specific (general praise/critique).
4. Barriers to Communication: Physical (noise, distance), Linguistic (jargon, language), Cultural, Emotional (fear, anger), Interpersonal (prejudice, ego).
5. The 7 Cs of Effective Communication: Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, Courteous.

UNIT 2: SELF-MANAGEMENT SKILLS - II
1. Stress: A state of mental or emotional strain resulting from adverse or demanding circumstances.
2. Stressors: Factors causing stress (exams, deadlines, family issues, illness).
3. ABC of Stress Management: A - Adversity (the stressful event), B - Beliefs (the way you perceive it), C - Consequences (actions and emotional outcome).
4. Stress Management Techniques: Physical exercise, Yoga, Meditation, Spending time in nature, Listening to music, Engaging in hobbies.
5. Ability to Work Independently: Requires Self-awareness (knowing strengths and weaknesses), Self-motivation (inner drive without external push), and Self-regulation (disciplined habit control).

UNIT 3: ICT SKILLS - II
1. Operating System (OS): System software managing computer hardware, files, memory, and software execution.
   - Types: Interactive (GUI), Real-Time (RTOS), Distributed, Single-User, Multi-User.
2. File Management: Directory paths, file naming conventions, file extensions (.txt, .docx, .ods, .jpg, .pdf), copying, moving, renaming, deleting, and restoring from Recycle Bin / Trash.
3. Computer Care & Maintenance:
   - Hardware: Cleaning keyboard/screen with microfiber cloth, avoiding food/liquids near PC, proper ventilation.
   - Software: Disk Cleanup (removing temporary files), Disk Defragmentation, updating software, running regular antivirus scans, safe data backup on cloud or external drive.

UNIT 4: ENTREPRENEURIAL SKILLS - II
1. Entrepreneur: A person who identifies a problem or need, develops an innovative solution, organizes resources, takes calculated financial risks, and establishes a commercial enterprise.
2. Characteristics: Passionate, self-confident, hard-working, creative, risk-taker, adaptable, continuous learner.
3. Role in Society: Generates employment, drives economic growth, fosters innovation, utilizes local resources, improves living standards.
4. Myths about Entrepreneurship:
   - Myth: Entrepreneurs are born, not made. (Reality: Entrepreneurial skills can be learned and nurtured).
   - Myth: You need a huge amount of capital to start. (Reality: Many successful startups begin with minimal funds).
   - Myth: Every startup must have a completely brand-new invention. (Reality: Innovating existing processes or services is highly effective).

UNIT 5: GREEN SKILLS - II
1. Sustainable Development: Development that meets the needs of the present generation without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
2. 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs): Formulated by the United Nations in 2015 to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure peace and prosperity by 2030 (e.g., No Poverty, Quality Education, Clean Water, Climate Action).
3. Green Economy: An economic system that results in improved human well-being and social equity while significantly reducing environmental risks and ecological scarcities.
4. Green Jobs: Decent jobs that contribute to preserving or restoring the environment (solar panel installation, e-waste recycling, sustainable farming, organic materials production).
5. Individual Actions: Conserving electricity and water, waste segregation, adopting 4Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Recover), minimizing single-use plastics, planting trees.
