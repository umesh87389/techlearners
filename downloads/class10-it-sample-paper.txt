CBSE Class 10 IT Practice Sample Paper

Time: 1 Hour
Marks: 30

Section A: Very Short Answer Questions
1. What is a style in digital documentation?
2. What is an absolute cell reference?
3. Define primary key.
4. What is a web application?
5. What is phishing?

Section B: Short Answer Questions
1. Write two benefits of using templates.
2. Explain the purpose of data validation in spreadsheets.
3. Differentiate between a field and a record.
4. Why are forms useful in a database?
5. Write two safe browsing practices.

Section C: Application Questions
1. A school needs personalised certificates for students. Explain how mail merge can help.
2. A spreadsheet contains marks for 40 students. Explain how to find total, average, highest mark, and lowest mark.
3. A library wants to store book records. Suggest suitable fields and one primary key.
