CBSE Class 9 AI Skill-Based Practice Paper

Time: 1 Hour
Marks: 30

Section A: Objective Questions
1. Write the full form of AI.
2. Name one daily-life AI example.
3. Write one benefit of active listening.
4. What is a SMART goal?
5. Write one cyber-safety rule.

Section B: Short Answer Questions
1. Explain why AI needs data.
2. Write two differences between verbal and non-verbal communication.
3. How can self-confidence help a student during presentation work?
4. Why should entrepreneurs listen to customer feedback?
5. Explain digital footprint with one example.

Section C: Case-Based Questions
1. A school wants to reduce lost library books. Suggest how data can help identify the problem.
2. A student is nervous before a class activity. Write four self-management steps that may help.
3. A group wants to make a useful school product. Identify a problem, users, possible solution, and one risk.
