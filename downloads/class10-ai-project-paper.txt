CBSE Class 10 AI Project-Based Practice Paper

Time: 1 Hour
Marks: 30

Section A: Objective Questions
1. What is problem scoping?
2. Name one data source for an AI project.
3. What is evaluation?
4. Write one green workplace habit.
5. What is self-regulation?

Section B: Short Answer Questions
1. Explain the role of data exploration.
2. Why should AI deployment be monitored?
3. Write two remedies for communication barriers.
4. How can planning reduce stress?
5. Why is sustainability important in technology use?

Section C: Case-Based Questions
1. A school wants to predict attendance risk. Define the problem and suggest three data fields.
2. An AI model gives unfair results for one group. What checks and improvements should be made?
3. Plan a green campaign for your classroom using three measurable actions.
