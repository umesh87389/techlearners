Editorial and Corrections Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Our Commitment

TechLearners aims to publish clear, useful, and original educational support material for school students. Resources are reviewed before publication and improved when feedback or curriculum needs change.

Content Updates

We may update materials to correct errors, improve explanations, reflect curriculum changes, add practice resources, or improve the website experience.

Academic Verification

Students should verify important academic details with official syllabi, textbooks, teachers, and examination authorities. TechLearners supplements those sources; it does not replace them.

Corrections

If you identify inaccurate, unclear, or outdated content, please send the page URL and a short explanation through the Contact Us page or email support.techlearners@gmail.com.
